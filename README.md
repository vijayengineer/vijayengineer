# Vijay Lakshminarayanan

**Researcher, AI Engineer, Technical Founder · agent evaluation · market design**

I research how rewards, risk controls and evaluations hold up once the people and agents they govern adapt, and I ship
the results. I built and launched [DIME](https://dime.rndm.io), a leveraged trading protocol on Robinhood Chain that
AI agents trade directly.

## Research

**Leverage as a Game: Complexity, incentive limits in trading** (sole author, 2026) ·
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24012800](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24012800) · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7683440)

An optimiser for the balance between rewards and revenue: a closed-form safe reward rate, proof that searching beyond it
is NP-hard, and tests on recorded Polymarket and Hyperliquid trades.
